# Dataset attribution and publication boundary

LoCoMo: Adyasha Maharana, Dong-Ho Lee, Sergey Tulyakov, Mohit Bansal,
Francesco Barbieri and Yuwei Fang, *Evaluating Very Long-Term Conversational
Memory of LLM Agents*, ACL 2024. [Official repository](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo).
The [upstream license](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo/blob/main/LICENSE.txt)
states Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International, verified
7 October 2026. That license does not become the HeurChain project license.
The current upstream license was consulted without opening the underlying dataset.
The frozen dataset revision/hash remain in completed result provenance.

This private publication contains research methods, aggregates, diagnostic
classifications and IDs, plus short excerpts already in historical research.
It contains no full conversation corpus, question/answer/gold bundles, retrieved
contexts, images, image URLs or sealed holdout content. The raw diagnostic row
fields and readable dump are omitted in publication copies. Any short dataset
phrases in historical reports retain this attribution and upstream terms; no
commercial redistribution rights or relicensing are asserted. Changes to the
publication copies are recorded in the root omissions manifest.
